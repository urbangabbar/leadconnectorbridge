A few things to know.

What is LeadConnector Bridge?

LeadConnector Bridge builds integrations for LeadConnector, including DocuSign, Community Agents, and a planned Google Sheets integration. Each product connects a different part of your business to your workspace.

Which products can I use today?

DocuSign is available, and Community Agents has been released. Google Sheets integration is coming soon. Contact our team to check the right setup and availability for your workspace.

Does this integrate with LeadConnector?

Yes. Our products are built around the LeadConnector ecosystem. DocuSign connects to contact records and workflows, Community Agents supports connected community portals, and Google Sheets sync is coming soon. We’ll help confirm the setup for your account.

Do I need my own accounts for the connected tools?

Yes. For example, the DocuSign integration connects to your own DocuSign account. The connector brings your tools into your workflows; it does not replace the underlying service or its subscription.

How do I get started?

Tell us which product you’re interested in and what you want to connect to LeadConnector. We’ll help confirm compatibility, talk through the setup, and recommend the next step.
